Inherits: Initializable, UUPSUpgradeable, OwnableUpgradeable, IDotnsRegistry

Title: Dot Registry

Upgradeable on-chain registry for hierarchical name ownership and resolution.

Stores ownership and resolver data for DotNS nodes. Authorisation is enforced strictly via node ownership, except for privileged ownership writes performed by a designated registrarController.

Note: security-contact: admin@parity.io
